## 📊 Task 1 – Juice Shop Price & Revenue Analysis

### Objective

To analyze the relationship between product price, bottles sold, and total revenue, and identify the price that generates the highest revenue.

### Analysis Performed

* Created a dataset containing **Price** and **Bottles Sold**.
* Calculated revenue using:

`Revenue = Price × Bottles Sold`

* Identified the highest revenue.
* Compared the prices that generated the highest revenue.
* Created a bar chart showing **Price vs Revenue**.

### Result

The highest revenue was **₹2,800**, achieved at both **₹35 and ₹40**.

However, ₹35 resulted in a higher sales volume, making it the better choice when considering both **revenue and customer demand**.

## 🍽️ Task 2 – Zomato Business Analysis

### Objective

To analyze restaurant, cuisine, pricing, ratings, and customer engagement data to identify useful business insights.

### Dataset

The dataset contains **123,657 records** and 12 columns, including:

* Restaurant Name
* Dining Rating
* Delivery Rating
* Dining Votes
* Delivery Votes
* Cuisine
* Place
* City
* Item Name
* Best Seller
* Votes
* Prices

### Data Cleaning

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

* Checked dataset information and data types.
* Identified missing values.
* Removed duplicate records.
* Analyzed categorical and numerical columns.
* Grouped and aggregated data to obtain business insights.

### Visualizations Created

1. **Top 10 Cities by Number of Restaurants**
2. **Top 5 Best Seller Categories**
3. **Average Price by City**

### Key Business Insights

| Business Question               | Finding                         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Average Dining Rating           | **3.82 / 5**                    |
| Most Common Cuisine             | **Beverages**                   |
| Most Common Cuisine Count       | **32,818**                      |
| Highest Average Delivery Rating | **Pune – 4.02 / 5**             |
| Highest Customer Engagement     | **Burger King – 525,173 votes** |
| Average Menu Item Price         | **₹243.69**                     |

### Overall Conclusion

The analysis shows that restaurant ratings, cuisine popularity, delivery performance, pricing, and customer engagement can provide valuable insights into restaurant business performance. These insights can help businesses improve their **pricing strategies, customer satisfaction, service quality, and overall performance**.
